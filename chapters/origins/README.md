# Origins

Bitcoin's ideas did not appear fully formed in 2008. This section covers the thirty years of cryptography, failed companies, mailing-list arguments, and unfinished proposals that came before it, and then covers Bitcoin's own first two years as a running system.

## What you need to know first

Nothing beyond general familiarity with how computers exchange data over a network. This section deliberately comes before [Cryptography](../cryptography/README.md) and [Distributed Systems](../distributed-systems/README.md). The goal here is historical and motivational context, not mechanism. Later sections explain the "how" behind ideas this section only names, such as hash functions, digital signatures, and consensus.

## Chapters

1. [Why Digital Cash Was Hard](./digital-cash.md): the double-spending problem and why every pre-Bitcoin system needed a trusted third party

3. [David Chaum and DigiCash](./digicash.md): blind signatures and the first real attempt at cryptographic digital cash

6. [Bit Gold](./bit-gold.md): Nick Szabo's proof-of-work-based digital scarcity design
7. [Who Was Satoshi Nakamoto?](./satoshi.md): documented fact versus speculation about Bitcoin's creator
8. [The Bitcoin Whitepaper](./bitcoin-whitepaper.md): a section-by-section walkthrough of the original paper
9. [The Genesis Block](./genesis-block.md): Bitcoin's first block, mined January 3, 2009
10. [Early Bitcoin History](./early-bitcoin.md): v0.1, the first transaction, early development, and Satoshi's departure
